## MHC Status

Primary Phase 3A analysis used GRCh37 chr6:25,000,000-34,000,000 exclusion. MHC-included MAGMA sensitivity was attempted but not estimable because full MHC-included gene-level analysis stalled in chr6/MHC computation.

## Allowed Claims

- The frozen transcriptomic axes can be described as replicated psoriasis molecular programs.
- Phase 3A did not detect robust inherited psoriasis susceptibility enrichment for F1/F2/F6/F7 CORE programs.
- Negative genetic anchoring suggests that these transcriptomic programs may reflect downstream tissue response, acquired inflammation, environmental effects, or disease-state adaptation rather than distinct germline susceptibility axes.

## Prohibited Claims

- Do not call F1/F2/F6/F7 genetically anchored molecular axes.
- Do not use EXTENDED F2 nominal sensitivity to claim genetic anchoring.
- Do not rename axes based on gene-level or gene-set genetics.
- Do not add GWAS-significant genes to frozen axis programs.
- Do not proceed to axis-specific eQTL colocalization, MR, LAVA, comorbidity GWAS, or drug prediction as if Phase 3A passed.

## Candidate Genes

Candidate genes in `results/phase3a/phase3b_candidate_genes.tsv` are exploratory future-review entries only. They are not eligible for Phase 3B colocalization as axis-anchored candidates unless the user explicitly pivots the project design.

## Phase 4 Eligibility

No retained axis is eligible for axis-specific multisystem comorbidity genetics. A separate, non-axis-specific psoriasis shared-genetics analysis could be designed later.
