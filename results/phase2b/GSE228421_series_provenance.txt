!Series_summary	"We obtained full-thickness skin biopsies from five individuals with severe psoriasis who were receiving risankizumab treatment. Skin biopsies were taken from lesional and non-lesional sites at baseline, and from lesional sites at day 3 and day 14 of therapy. Single-cell RNA sequencing was performed using a 10x Genomics platform."
