# Major Language Changes

| Section | Before | After | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Abstract | Long explanatory abstract with multiple caveats | Seven compact result-led sentences | Higher information density and clearer gap-result-conclusion sequence |
| Introduction | Four paragraphs with repeated map/layer explanation | Three compact paragraphs | Removed repeated framing and preserved one clean gap statement |
| Results 1 | Method-led clustering narrative | Discrete molecular endotypes were not reproducible | Result-first syntax |
| Results 2 | Contextualization phrasing repeated | Consistent cellular and spatial associations | More concrete terminology |
| Results 3 | Two-paragraph explanation of genetic decision | One compact subsection | Removed duplicate caveat |
| Results 4 | Subjective cleanest/behaved-as language | QC-passing and near-neighbor phrasing | More technical and less conversational |
| Results 5 | Aim-led LAVA explanation | Direct local correlation counts | Factual Results style |
| Results 6 | Interpretive regulatory title | Restricted shared regulatory signals | Better match to limited coloc support |
| Discussion P1 | Layered model developed over many sentences | Punchy two-scale summary | Stronger opening |
| Discussion P2 | Defensive explanation of failed endotypes | Tissue-state versus germline subtype logic | Clearer biological interpretation |
| Discussion P3 | CAD caveats spread across paragraph | Architecture claim separated from mediator claim | Less reviewer-response style |
| Discussion P4 | IBD explanation with practical caveats | Directional heterogeneity and QC sensitivity | Tighter claim boundary |
| Discussion P5 | Method sequence repeated | Evidence-scale hierarchy | Interpretation rather than methods recap |
| Discussion P6 | Long limitations list | Three bounded limitation classes | More disciplined close |

| Table 2 | Long final interpretation cells | Short evidence-level phrases | Better table readability |
